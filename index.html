1st Naval Expeditionary Force

Anvil Joint Task Force's Naval Branch

The 1st Naval Expeditionary Force (1st NEF) serves as the naval branch of Anvil Joint Task Force. It is organized around maritime operations, naval support, and amphibious capabilities within the Joint Task Force's organizational structure.

This knowledge base serves as the central reference repository for the 1st NEF, containing documentation on its organization, units, personnel, equipment, operations, and regulations. It is intended to provide members with an accessible and consistent source of information regarding the branch and its activities.
